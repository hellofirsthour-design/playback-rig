https://github.com/sfzinstruments/DrumGizmo.DRSKit (DRSKit v1.0).

Changes made by Playback Rig (scripts/make-drum-kit.py): each hit's 13 microphones were mixed
to stereo, close mics on other drums turned down, hits sorted by loudness, tails trimmed,
stored as 16-bit FLAC at full scale with a gain per hit.
